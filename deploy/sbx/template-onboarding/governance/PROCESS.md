# The template process: onboarding, gating, provenance, activation

> **Preview.** The template process is implemented but not yet declared Stable; interfaces and limits
> may change. Known limits: [the register](GAPS-AND-ASSUMPTIONS.md).

Written while the process is built: steps marked _(planned)_ do not exist yet. **Self-serve** (register
D12, the [design](../../../../docs/specs/2026-11-03-template-lifecycle-and-provenance-design.md) 0.4):
a client onboards and activates its own templates; policy, not an approver, gates them; admins set
the policy and the quotas.

Each step names who does it, the tool, the rules it applies ([`GATING-RULES.md`](GATING-RULES.md))
and the record it leaves. Provenance is hosted in the control plane; the workload plane (the KVM
host, the worker) only produces it, and every record must be verifiable offline (register D7, D8).
